# 3. Delegate Smart, Micromanage Smarter 🎯
<!-- ![bg right:39% 200%] -->
![w:1024 center](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1529699211952-734e80c4d42b)

---

### The Balance:  
1. Delegate low-impact tasks  
2. Assign high-impact tasks others excel at  
3. Own what only you can do  

### Micromanage Wisely:  
- Step in when it’s critical  
- Communicate clearly  
- Oversee strategically  

> "Hire talent, guide them, fix their drift, and pave the path."

- Were they the difference-maker?  

> "I hire winners." —Billion-dollar founder
